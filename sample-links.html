Visit Logger: sample link list

A generated list like this one is what the extension is built for: open 20 employer and job pages a few at a time, remember which ones you've reviewed, and point out careers links, pay, clearance, and work mode on each page. See README.md for the details.

Try it

  1. Open this file in a new window. The queue keeps at most 5 tabs open in the window, counting this one.
  2. Right-click the page and choose Start to continuously open not visited links. Four tabs open in the background.
  3. Look through a tab and close it. The next link opens in its place, until all 20 have opened. Stop opening links in the same menu stops the queue.
  4. On a company home page, the panel in the top corner lists the careers links it found. Click one, or press Ctrl+Alt+J to follow the first. On a job posting, click a colored count in the panel to scroll to its next match.
  5. Press F9 in a tab to put an "R" (resume sent) on its entry.
  6. Start the queue on this page again: nothing opens, because every link was marked visited when its tab opened. To replay a link, right-click in its tab and choose Unmark Site as Visited.

The visit log of a temporary add-on is discarded when you remove it or restart Firefox. To keep it, set extensions.webextensions.keepStorageOnUninstall and extensions.webextensions.keepUuidOnUninstall to true in about:config.

How the links are marked up

Only anchors with data-visit-open are queued. Every other link on the page, such as "careers" and "search" below, is ignored.

<a href="https://redwirespace.com"
   data-visit-open                   queue this link
   data-visit-key="redwirespace.com" record the visit under this key
   data-visit-mark="auto">           mark it visited as soon as its tab opens
  Redwire</a>

Without data-visit-key, the visit is recorded under the link's own host. Without data-visit-mark="auto", it is recorded only when you press F9, so an unreviewed link opens again next time. The queue never opens a link whose key is already in the visit log.
